Account policy

  • Personal email addresses are accepted.
  • Email confirmation should remain enabled.
  • Never share administrator passwords.
  • Only three approved admins should receive admin roles.

Data & privacy

  • Collect only necessary student information.
  • Keep the service role key on the server.
  • Use HTTPS in production.
  • Obtain appropriate institutional approval before official deployment.
